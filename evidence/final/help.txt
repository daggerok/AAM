MAX_FETCHES=0: full pass/reset cursor; positive: resume bounded batches.
TICKERS: space/comma/semicolon allowlist, AND with every filter; unselected funds retained.
Ranges: inclusive min:max / min: / :max / :; AUM K/M/B/T or nano/micro/small/mid/large.
PERFORMANCE_*: annualized for 3Y+; TOTAL_RETURN_*: cumulative. Missing values fail bounded filters.
REQUEST_SLEEP: seconds between request starts PER lane; CONCURRENCY: parallel fund workers.
MAX_RETRIES: retries after first request (network/408/425/429/5xx only).
HOLDINGS_PAGE_SIZE/HISTORY_PAGE_SIZE: generated rows/page. HISTORY_RANGE: max or Ny (old rows retained).
SEC_UA: identifying User-Agent/contact, EDGAR_FALLBACK: holdings only. SKIP_AAM/SKIP_YAHOO: opt-out, retain cache.
VERBOSE=1: retry/fallback detail. No dry-run: actual CLI writes data.
Examples:
  TICKERS="SPDV PFLD CLOC" VERBOSE=1 bun scripts/update-data.ts
  MAX_FETCHES=3 bun scripts/update-data.ts
  AUM="10M:2B" TER=":0.5" bun scripts/update-data.ts
  PERFORMANCE_1Y="15:" bun scripts/update-data.ts
